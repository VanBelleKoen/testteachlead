Outside of work, my curiosity tends to find different outlets.

Reading has always been one of them. I love stories because they are one of the most interesting ways to understand people. A good book lets me step into a perspective I would never have experienced myself, and explore worlds that only existed in someone else's mind.

Writing is the other side of that same fascination. I enjoy taking ideas, feelings, and observations that are still unclear and turning them into something tangible. There is something almost magical about creating a world from nothing but words and convincing someone else to briefly believe in it. I have taken part in NaNoWriMo several times, mostly because I enjoy the challenge of sitting down and creating something where nothing existed before.

Archery appeals to a completely different part of my brain. It is quiet, repetitive, and precise. There is no hiding behind excuses when you release the arrow. The moment it leaves the bow, you already know what happened. You know whether your stance was right, whether your focus was there, whether you did the work beforehand. The arrow reaching the target is almost just confirmation.

I also enjoy contributing to open source projects. There is something satisfying about people building together without the usual boundaries of companies and job descriptions. Sharing knowledge, improving something together, and creating value for people you may never meet is a powerful idea.

Different activities, same pattern: I am drawn to things that let me understand, create, improve, or solve something. Apparently, even my hobbies cannot escape the question of "why".
