# arxcus

## Overview

arxcus is a billing tool proof-of-concept developed for managing invoices and payments within the Cronos group ecosystem. This comprehensive solution addresses the complex billing needs of a large technology group with multiple subsidiaries and diverse service offerings.

## Key Features

- **Invoice management**: Comprehensive invoice creation, tracking, and management
- **Payment processing**: Integrated payment handling and reconciliation
- **Multi-entity support**: Billing across multiple Cronos group companies
- **Automated workflows**: Streamlined billing processes and approvals
- **Financial reporting**: Detailed reporting and analytics capabilities
- **Integration capabilities**: Seamless integration with existing financial systems

## Impact

This proof-of-concept demonstrated the potential for streamlining billing operations across the entire Cronos group, potentially reducing administrative overhead and improving financial process efficiency.

## Technologies Used

- Financial software frameworks
- Payment processing APIs
- Database management systems
- Reporting and analytics tools
- Enterprise integration platforms

## My Role

I was responsible for testing the complex billing workflows, payment processing accuracy, and multi-entity scenarios, ensuring the proof-of-concept met the rigorous requirements of enterprise financial management.
