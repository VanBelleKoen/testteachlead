# Inclusief Ondernemen (PIO)

## Overview

Inclusief Ondernemen is an initiative that promotes inclusive entrepreneurship and business practices within the Flemish economy. This platform supports businesses in developing more inclusive approaches to employment and operations.

## Key Features

- **Inclusive business guidance**: Resources for implementing inclusive practices
- **Entrepreneurship support**: Tools and guidance for diverse entrepreneurs
- **Best practices sharing**: Platform for sharing successful inclusion strategies
- **Policy alignment**: Integration with Flemish inclusion policies
- **Business development**: Support for inclusive business growth

## Impact

This initiative helps create a more inclusive business environment in Flanders, supporting entrepreneurs from diverse backgrounds and encouraging existing businesses to adopt more inclusive practices.

## Technologies Used

- Content management systems
- Business resource platforms
- Information sharing portals
- User engagement tools
