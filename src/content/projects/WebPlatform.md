# Web Platform (Digitaal Vlaanderen)

## Overview

The Web Platform is a comprehensive framework developed by Digitaal Vlaanderen that provides a unified foundation for building and managing digital services in the public sector. This platform enhances user experience and accessibility while standardizing development practices across government digital services.

## Key Features

- **Unified development framework**: Standardized tools and components for government services
- **User experience optimization**: Consistent design patterns and accessibility standards
- **Service management**: Centralized platform for deploying and managing digital services
- **Component library**: Reusable UI components and design systems
- **Accessibility compliance**: Built-in accessibility features meeting government standards
- **Performance optimization**: Optimized platform for high-traffic government services

## Impact

This platform accelerates the development of government digital services while ensuring consistency, accessibility, and high quality across all public sector digital touchpoints.

## Technologies Used

- Modern web development frameworks
- Component-based architecture
- Accessibility testing tools
- Performance monitoring systems
- Content management systems

## My Role

I was involved in comprehensive testing of the platform's components and frameworks, ensuring cross-browser compatibility, accessibility compliance, and performance optimization for government service delivery.
