# Kwaliteitsdatabank

## Overview

The Kwaliteitsdatabank is an open-data registry managed by the Flemish Department of Work, Economy, Innovation, and Social Economy (WEWIS). It maintains a comprehensive listing of service providers who are officially recognized for meeting stringent quality and registration standards.

## Key Features

- **Open data registry**: Publicly accessible database
- **Quality assurance**: Verified service providers only
- **Transparency**: Clear visibility into provider qualifications
- **Government recognition**: Official validation of standards
- **Public access**: Available to institutions and funding platforms

## Impact

This registry ensures transparency in the marketplace and enables both public institutions and funding platforms to make informed decisions when selecting service providers, ultimately raising the standard of services across the region.

## Technologies Used

- Open data platform architecture
- Database management systems
- Web-based public interface
- Data validation and verification tools
